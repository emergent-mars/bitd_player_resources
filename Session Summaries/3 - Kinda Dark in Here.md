11/14/26 - Eigls and Finch

Eigls and Finch secured a loyal fence by sabotaging a smuggling route of the Vultures. They collapsed a section of the underground canal they use to transport goods.

The bomb's detonation mechanism failed to activate at first, requiring Finch to shoot at it and trigger the explosion. It brought down the already crumbling ceiling and created a sinkhole from the surface, falling on the Vultures' ship. The waves from the debris slammed the crew's ship into a wall and it sank. As the two started to climb the ladder out of the nearest hatch, the air grew cold around them. Both realized it was a ghost and hauled ass up into the abandoned apartment building above. On the ground floor, the ghost shut and locked every door, and supernaturally blocked the windows so they couldn't be broken. Finch lost his shit and ended up getting shot in the side by her own ricocheting bullet off the window. 

The ghost, who later identified herself as Mara, was a slender Iruvian woman wearing a red sash and rapier with a white leather wrapping around the handle. Her faced was permanently stained with tears and she limped when she tried to run.

She begged them to help her and said she had to find what happened to her body, and said "they took it", and that her body was still alive. Eigls copied some of the sigils drawn on her neck and down her spine, but she couldn't alter her outfit in any way including lifting her shirt to show the rest (or using her rapier).

Eigls promised to help her and the two left.

Crew moved their relationship with the Fog Hounds back to neutral, and the Vultures to a -2, and gained a loyal fence.
